# Bumpus house sparrow survival data

Morphological measurements and overwinter survival of house sparrows
(*Passer domesticus*) collected by Hermon Bumpus in 1898 after a severe
winter storm in Providence, Rhode Island.

## Usage

``` r
bumpus
```

## Format

A data frame with 136 rows and 11 variables:

- sex:

  Sex of the bird (`male` or `female`).

- survival:

  Survival indicator: `1` if the bird survived, `0` otherwise.

- total_length:

  Total length (mm).

- wingspread:

  Alar extent / wingspread (mm).

- weight:

  Body weight (g).

- head_length:

  Length of head and beak (mm).

- humerus:

  Length of humerus (in).

- femur:

  Length of femur (in).

- tibiotarsus:

  Length of tibiotarsus (in).

- skull_width:

  Width of skull (in).

- sternum:

  Length of sternum / keel (in).

## Source

Bumpus, H. C. (1899). The elimination of the unfit as illustrated by the
introduced sparrow, *Passer domesticus*. *Biological Lectures, Marine
Biological Laboratory, Woods Hole*, 209-226.
