# lande: Standardised Analyses of Natural Selection Data

Estimates selection differentials and linear, quadratic and
correlational selection gradients on relative fitness following Lande
and Arnold (1983)
[doi:10.1111/j.1558-5646.1983.tb00236.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1558-5646.1983.tb00236.x)
, with p-values from the logistic model for survival and from a Poisson
or negative binomial model for counts. Fits cubic spline fitness
functions as in Schluter (1988)
[doi:10.1111/j.1558-5646.1988.tb02507.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1558-5646.1988.tb02507.x)
and two-trait fitness surfaces drawn only where there are data, and
computes adaptive landscapes of mean fitness against the population mean
for one or two traits, by period and for several groups on one surface.
Includes bootstrap intervals, a combined results table and a 'shiny'
app.

## Data

Each dataset has its own help page, with its source:
[`bumpus`](https://human-augment-analytics.github.io/lande/dev/reference/bumpus.md),
[`crescent_pond_pupfish`](https://human-augment-analytics.github.io/lande/dev/reference/crescent_pond_pupfish.md)
and
[`little_lake_pupfish`](https://human-augment-analytics.github.io/lande/dev/reference/crescent_pond_pupfish.md),
[`finch_yearly`](https://human-augment-analytics.github.io/lande/dev/reference/finch_yearly.md)
and
[`finch_community`](https://human-augment-analytics.github.io/lande/dev/reference/finch_community.md).
The pupfish and finch data are also in `inst/extdata` as CSV files.
